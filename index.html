About Maharashtra

Maharashtra is one of India's most diverse states. It is famous for its rich history, beautiful landscapes, forts, culture and traditional food.

From the busy streets of Mumbai to the peaceful hills of Mahabaleshwar, Maharashtra offers many places to explore.

Maharashtra has many historic forts that represent the state's rich history.
